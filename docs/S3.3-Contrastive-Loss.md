# S3.3 — Contrastive Loss

## Purpose

S3.3 adds the metric-learning objective used to train the shared Siamese embedding model. It is intentionally independent of the Siamese architecture, pair generation, optimizer, and retrieval pipeline.

## Contract

`ContrastiveLoss(margin=1.0)` accepts:

- `embedding_a`: `[B, D]`
- `embedding_b`: `[B, D]`
- `labels`: `[B]`
- returns one scalar mean loss.

Project label convention:

- `0` = positive pair, same product
- `1` = negative pair, different products

Pairs are product-level: two images from different products remain negative even when their category is identical.

## Formula

For Euclidean distance `d = ||embedding_a - embedding_b||_2` and margin `m`:

`L = (1-y) * d^2 + y * max(m-d, 0)^2`

Positive pairs minimize squared distance. Negative pairs are penalized only while their distance is below the margin.

## Numerical stability

Distance calculations are performed in float32. The negative hinge term uses `relu(margin - distance)` before squaring, avoiding manual branching and preventing negative hinge values. Inputs and the final loss are checked for finite values. Invalid labels, shapes, empty batches, and margins are rejected explicitly.

## Scope boundaries

S3.3 does not modify `SiameseNetwork`, `CustomCNNEncoder`, `EmbeddingHead`, retrieval similarity, Top-K retrieval, pair sampling, augmentation, optimizer configuration, or training/evaluation results. Pair sampling and actual metric-learning training remain later tasks.
