# S3.9 — Ranking

## Objective

Implement the explicit product-ranking stage after S3.8 similarity search.

## Contract

```text
S3.8 ProductSearchResult
        ↓
     ProductRanker
        ↓
RankedProductSearchResult
```

The ranker sorts unique product candidates by raw cosine similarity in descending order:

```text
Similarity ↓
```

Ranks are one-based (`1 ... K`).

## Deterministic tie-breaking

When two products have the same similarity, `product_id` ascending is used as the secondary key. This makes ranking reproducible and independent of gallery insertion order.

## Responsibilities

S3.9 owns:

- product ordering
- one-based rank assignment
- deterministic tie-breaking
- validation of unique product IDs and finite raw similarities
- preserving product/image metadata

S3.9 does **not**:

- calculate embeddings
- perform similarity search
- change the raw similarity value
- convert similarity to a display score
- interpret similarity as probability
- evaluate retrieval metrics
- compare Baseline and Siamese models

Those responsibilities belong to S3.8, S3.10, S3.11 and S3.12 respectively.

## Immutability

`ProductSearchResult` is not mutated. Ranking creates a new immutable `RankedProductSearchResult` and copies the candidate metadata into immutable ranked records.

## Compatibility

The existing S2.7 `TopKRetriever`, `RetrievalResult`, and baseline pipeline are untouched. S3.9 operates on the new S3.8 product-level contract.

Policy identifier: `s3.9-deterministic-product-ranking-v1`.
